## Description (4000)

Glance turns screenshots into images worth sharing — in two keystrokes.

Press a shortcut, pick an area or a window, and Glance frames your capture on a beautiful background with padding, rounded corners and a soft shadow. Add arrows, boxes, labels or numbered steps, pixelate anything private, and copy the result straight to your clipboard. It's ready to paste into Slack, a doc, a bug report or a post.

CAPTURE ANYTHING
• Area capture: drag to select any region, on any of your displays
• Window capture: click a window to capture it on its own, even the parts hidden behind other windows, with transparent corners
• Full-screen capture of the display under your pointer
• Custom global shortcuts for each mode

PIXEL-PERFECT SELECTION
• The screen freezes the moment you press the shortcut, so menus, tooltips and hover states stay put
• Magnifier loupe with pixel coordinates
• Crosshair guides and snapping to window edges
• Live size readout as you drag

PRESENTATION-READY IN ONE STEP
• Hand-picked gradient backgrounds, plain white or transparent
• Adjustable padding and corner radius
• Your style is remembered for the next screenshot

MARK IT UP
• Arrows, rectangles, text, numbered steps and pixelation
• Six colours
• Move, resize, recolour and edit annotations after drawing
• Full undo and redo
• Real pixelation that makes sensitive text genuinely unreadable

SHARE IT
• Copy to the clipboard, or save a Retina-sharp PNG to Downloads or any folder you choose
• Transparent PNGs, shadow included
• Reopen any of your last 10 captures from the menu bar, annotations and all

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Glance has no network access at all. No accounts, no uploads, no analytics. Your screenshots never leave your Mac unless you share them yourself.

Native Swift app built with ScreenCaptureKit, SwiftUI and AppKit. It lives in your menu bar and stays out of the way until you need it.

## App Review Information

**Sign-in required:** No.

**Notes:**

Glance is a menu bar app, so it has no Dock icon or main window. After launch, look for its icon (viewfinder corners with a dot) in the menu bar.

To test:
1. Click the menu bar icon and choose "Capture Area" (or press Shift-Command-1).
2. macOS asks for Screen Recording permission. Allow Glance in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording, then reopen the app if macOS asks.
3. Drag to select an area, or click a window to capture it. The editor opens.
4. Use the toolbar to add arrows, text and other annotations, then click Copy or Save.

Screen Recording is used only to capture the screen when the user asks for a screenshot. The app has no network access and collects no data.
